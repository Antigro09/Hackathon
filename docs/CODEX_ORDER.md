## P0-1: LEARN panel (45 min)

- New route `#/learn` (or panel on Home per v2 plan: "one short How-this-works panel" — implement BOTH: panel on Measure under the result card, full route as its own tab).
- Content:
  1. Tone player — Web Audio oscillator buttons: **63 Hz, 125 Hz, 500 Hz, 1 kHz, 8 kHz** (5 buttons, maximum gain 0.15, quiet initial volume, stop button, no autoplay). Stop and release playback on navigation or unmount. Playback must not run during a measurement or be presented as a microphone calibration. The historical `mic_spike.html` is research material; reuse remains subject to the event's organizer ruling described in the corrected plan.
  2. A-vs-C explainer: two short cards, plain English. Copy: "A-weighting reduces the contribution of low frequencies more than C-weighting. TonalWatch applies these two frequency weightings to the same captured digital samples. These are uncalibrated weighted digital levels, not sound-pressure levels or a direct measure of discomfort. A speaker or microphone may reproduce low frequencies poorly."
  3. Gap explainer: "C-weighted digital level minus A-weighted digital level describes the captured spectrum. A larger difference can suggest low-frequency contribution. It does not by itself establish a tone, identify its source, measure what someone feels, or determine legal compliance. The sustained-peak indicator is a separate experimental check."
  4. The honesty line, verbatim: "Uncalibrated screening only. No source identification or legal-compliance determination."
- Acceptance: playback requires a click and stops correctly; route reachable in nav; explanatory copy remains readable in print. A pure 1 kHz tone and the existing low-frequency broadband fixture demonstrate why the gap and sustained-peak result are independent. Keep demonstrations labeled synthetic through any export.

## P0-2: MAP tab with seeded local data (60 min)

- New route `#/map`. Bundle Leaflet through npm, with no library CDN. An OpenStreetMap tile basemap still requires network access; bundling Leaflet does not make tiles available offline. Retain required attribution and comply with the selected tile provider's usage policy. Do not bulk-download tiles to manufacture an offline cache. Offline, display an explicit basemap-unavailable notice and keep pins accessible in an attributed bundled schematic or a readable list. A fully bundled geographic asset is an alternative only when its license and attribution permit that use.
- Data: `src/data/readings.ts` — static array of 12–15 **synthetic demonstration records**. Every record must carry explicit provenance: `inputKind: 'synthetic'`, a synthetic label, illustrative coordinates, example weighted digital levels/gap, fictional device and time, and fictional conditions. Label invented devices and timestamps as examples, not hardware validation or field observations.
  - Use approximate illustrative positions in the Ashburn/Sterling/Chantilly area, without implying a real household, facility, measurement location, or source attribution. A per-pin label such as **"Illustrative site N — synthetic data; no measurement collected"** must remain visible in popups, lists, and any export. A global demo banner alone is insufficient.
  - Example gaps may span 8–24 dB, with two above the prototype 15 dB threshold. Pin color may represent only that **low-frequency screen**: grey for "not indicated" and amber for "low-frequency contribution suggested." Preserve any separate peak result; the gap must never produce a "tonal signature" verdict. Include quality/inconclusive states. No red, violation, source-identity, or promised engineer-action language.
  - Omit the proposed 90 dB news pin unless its original source and measurement context are independently verified. If later included, make it a distinct **externally reported observation**, link the verified source, retain reported units and available instrument/time/context, and state that TonalWatch did not collect or validate it. Never merge it into browser digital readings or synthetic values.
- Pin popup: explicit origin, weighted digital units (dB re digital RMS 1) and uncalibrated status for browser/example values, plus clearly labeled illustrative or user-supplied context. External observations retain their original reported units and separate provenance. Preserve synthetic, imported-file, and microphone provenance separately.
- A "Live reading" button: "Add current result to map (local preview)." Require the user to manually supply or select the actual recording location and confirm its association with the active observation. Do not infer coordinates, silently use a seeded site, or request GPS. If the location is unknown, keep the observation unplaced in the list. Synthetic inputs remain synthetic; imported files require their own user-supplied recording location. Append only in memory, with "local preview, resets on reload" visible; no backend or shared claims.
- Acceptance: test online rendering, then test the local production preview with an empty tile cache and external networking blocked while the local app remains reachable. Missing tiles must not break pins/list access or labels; this does not promise that an uncached hosted app can boot without a network. Verify clickable pins, provenance on every seeded record, manual-location handling, reset behavior, and no console errors. Disclose that online tile requests reach the tile provider; audio and observation fields stay local.

## P0-3: Longer capture mode (30 min)

- On Measure: duration selector `5s / 15s / 30s` (default 5). This needs coordinated implementation and tests: the current microphone collector is fixed at five seconds. Update actual sample collection, progress/cancellation/interruption handling, DSP input assumptions, result metadata, spectrum provenance, and printable/text reports together. Keep imported-file crop policy explicit instead of changing it silently.
- Analyze the actual selected samples, derive frame count from those samples, and state the real duration in the report. A longer browser capture does **not** establish compliance with any county's measurement procedure. Omit "meets county minimum" or equivalent claims; professional methods involve requirements beyond duration.
- Preserve the AI experiment's distinct five-second contract. For an eligible longer recording, explicitly copy its **first five seconds** for AI, resample that copy to 48 kHz, and retain the existing five-second observation plus five-second zero-padding contract. Label the AI segment and its actual source metadata separately from the full DSP recording. Do not pass 15/30-second metadata or PCM into the existing worker, which rejects it, or imply that hints cover the entire longer recording. The Colab probe uses the same five-second window; changing that window requires a separate, revalidated model/preprocessing contract.
- Acceptance: verify 5/15/30-second sample counts, frame math, progress, cancellation and interruptions, report/spectrum duration, and unchanged five-second fixture results. Verify AI cropping, metadata, cancellation, and first-five-second parity without changing the deterministic DSP result.

## P0-4: Buddy-check chip (45 min)

- On Measure result panel: "Compare with previous" — stores the previous result's gap and provenance in memory, shows `Δgap vs previous capture: +4.2 dB` when both results have valid numeric gaps. Mark synthetic comparisons clearly and do not turn unavailable or inconclusive values into zero.
- Explanation tooltip: "This is the observed difference between these two captures. Changes in sound, placement, microphone frequency response, or processing can change the gap. It is not a calibration offset and does not make devices interchangeable." Only constant gain on otherwise identical unclipped samples cancels in C−A; device response shaping does not.
- Keep the comparison descriptive and session-local. Two devices require deliberate entry or import of the other observation with its provenance; do not imply that an in-memory previous-result chip links two phones automatically. Do not pool, correct, rank, or normalize map readings across devices from one comparison.
- Acceptance: verify signed differences and provenance, first-capture/invalid-result states, and reset behavior. No device calibration or field-accuracy claim.

## P1 (only after approved P0 features pass): 60 Hz spectrum exhibit
- Learn panel addition: "Look for a peak near 60 Hz." With tone playback stopped, capture a sample and inspect the spectrum near 60 Hz and 120 Hz. Use 15 seconds only if longer capture has been implemented and verified; otherwise retain the supported five-second mode.
- Caption: "A peak near 60 Hz is a frequency observation. Several acoustic or recording-chain sources can produce it; this does not identify the power grid, equipment, a building, or transmission through a wall." Display actual FFT/bin resolution and avoid claiming that absence rules out a sound or that one recording validates microphone sensitivity.
