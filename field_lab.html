FIELD LAB — standalone spike instrument

Uncalibrated consumer mic. Gap = C−A on captured signal; honest verdict = relative. This file is separate from the app — it's the experiment bench.

Protocol (every capture)

  1. Stand still. Walk = finding spots, never measuring them.
  2. Three captures per spot, ~30 s apart (E1 uses the duration selector).
  3. Log spot, in/out, distance, what you hear. Auto-logged below.
  4. Gap ≥ 15 → freeze, take THREE confirmations.

The tiers

TIER 1 — signature hunt (E5). Kitchen exhaust / rooftop AHU · gym air system · stairwell AC unit · exterior transformer pads (grey cabinets, HIGH VOLTAGE) · elevator machine room. Hunt: fans + transformers = the 50–250 Hz tonal band, same equipment class as data center cooling.
TIER 2 — controlled experiments. E1 duration: stairwell, 5s vs 15s vs 30s ×3. E2 distance: one loud source, 2m / 8m / 20m — does gap hold while level drops (mile-away physics)? E3 masking: quiet corner alone vs same corner + talking person. E4 walk test: stationary ×3 vs one slow walk past the same spot — compare spread.
TIER 3 — free exhibits. 60 Hz mains check (quiet room, nothing playing, find 60/120 Hz peak) · railroad train pass (log it, exclude as extraneous) · buddy check (two devices, same spot — Δgap = device offset).
