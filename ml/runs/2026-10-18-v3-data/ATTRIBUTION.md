# Source and attribution notices

This source-only audit redistributes metadata, source-preparation code and a small annotation file. It does not redistribute audio. Repository software licensing does not replace dataset or individual recording licenses. The exact per-record `attribution`, `license`, `source_url`, creator metadata and dataset citation are retained in `final-data.json` and the component manifests.

**BSD35k-CS:** Panagiota Anastasopoulou and Frederic Font Corbera, Universitat Pompeu Fabra, *BSD35k-CS (Broad Sound Dataset 35k – Crowd Sourced)*, Zenodo, 2026, [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19187100](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19187100). Dataset metadata is [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); each audio clip retains its original Freesound uploader's declared license. This subset excludes BY-NC, SA, Sampling+ and other unapproved audio licenses, and conservatively excludes conflicting creator restrictions found in the available text. Creator-specified credit details remain in the original descriptions, including requests that differ from a profile name. Our derived sound-family labels, group reservations and first-five-second crops are TonalWatch preprocessing decisions, not publisher labels or author endorsements.

**To bee or not to bee:** Inês Nolasco and Emmanouil Benetos, Queen Mary University of London, *To bee or not to bee*, Zenodo, 2018, [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.1321278](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1321278), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The preserved `beeAnnotations.mlf` is the exact published human annotation file; it has not been repaired or relabeled. Our exclusions, hive grouping, first-channel five-second extraction and 48 kHz conversion are separate derivations. OSBH citizen-science and NU-Hive collection lineage is retained, without claiming that the dataset authors recorded every underlying source personally.

**FSD50K:** Eduardo Fonseca, Xavier Favory, Jordi Pons, Frederic Font and Xavier Serra, *FSD50K: An Open Dataset of Human-Labeled Sound Events*, [Zenodo release](https://zenodo.org/records/4060432), [primary dataset repository](https://github.com/MTG/FSD50K). The selected source recordings retain the individual Freesound creator credits and CC0/CC BY deeds linked in each record. The pinned [Fhrozen/FSD50k mirror](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Fhrozen/FSD50k/tree/ccf1acaff12f3f4a4c10052dddafbb6d22152c9b) supplies selective WAV access; the original metadata archives and per-record hashes establish lineage. Our final-audit reservation and first-five-second conversion are derived partitions/preprocessing, not an official publisher split beyond the recorded original `eval` membership.

The final 416 recording records declare 278 [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), 121 [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and 17 [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) audio licenses. Preserve these creator/source/changes notices with subsequent use or artifact distribution; metadata licenses do not silently authorize a conflicting recording license. No MIMII, MIMII-DUE, MIMII-DG, NC-licensed audio or IVBEE sources are included in this final cohort.
